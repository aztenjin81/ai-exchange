# Food Profile

## Texture

- Prefers semi-soft. Mushy food (baked beans, etc.) causes nausea.
- Rice is meh unless heavily buttered.

## Yes

- Pizza (pepperoni)
- Chicken tenders
- Fries
- Spaghetti
- Hot dogs
- Grilled cheese
- Nachos (cheese only — NOT supreme)
- Bacon
- Scrambled eggs
- Grilled chicken
- Steak (grilled)
- Fish sticks
- Box mac & cheese (Velveeta or Kraft)
- Pancakes / waffles

## No

- Mexican food / tacos
- Burgers
- Wings
- Stir fry
- Sushi
- Ribs / BBQ
- Burrito bowls
- Corn dogs
- Meatball subs
- Chef salads
- Roasted vegetables
- Baked mac & cheese
- Supreme nachos
- Most sandwiches (PB&J is the exception — but Ian has peanut allergy)

## Notes

- Ian has a peanut allergy — no peanut products in the house
- Water measured in oz, not ml
